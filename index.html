Carolina Curvale, PhD

Associate Professor, Department of Political Studies · FLACSO Ecuador
Google Scholar ORCID SSRN LinkedIn

Carolina Curvale is a political scientist (PhD NYU) and Associate Professor in the Department of Political Studies at FLACSO Ecuador. Her research spans comparative politics, political economy, and Latin American politics.

Her current work examines the political economy of AI in technology-taker societies. This extends a long-standing program on institutional fragility and democratic stability in Latin America, grounded in an original 19-country dataset spanning two centuries of political instability and an early collaboration with Adam Przeworski. Parallel streams address inequality, electoral behavior, and political institutions.

Her work is comparative, empirically grounded, and quantitatively oriented, combining formal theory, survey experiments, and original historical and microdata.
